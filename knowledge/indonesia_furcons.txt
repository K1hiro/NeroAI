Title: Indonesian Furry Events — PAWAI and IWAG
Research date: 2026-10-17

## What is a furry convention?
A furry convention, often called a furcon, brings together people
interested in anthropomorphic animal characters: animal characters
with human traits. Activities can include art, panels, games,
performances, and community gatherings.

## PAWAI
PAWAI is an Indonesian furry event.
Its official website lists the 2026 edition as "Gods Paradise",
with event dates of October 3–4, 2026, at Hotel Grand Istana Rama
in Kuta, Bali, Indonesia.

These dates refer specifically to the 2026 edition. They should
not be treated as the dates or location of every PAWAI event.

Source:
https://pawai.id/

## What does IWAG mean?
IWAG stands for Indonesia Weekend Anthro Gathering.
It is an Indonesian furry convention focused on community,
creativity, and shared interest in anthropomorphic animals.
Its official description mentions workshops, talk shows,
games, and art exhibitions.

Source:
https://iwag.furries.id/en/about/

## IWAG history
IWAG developed from Indonesia Winter Furmeet, abbreviated IWFM.
According to its organizers, the Winter Furmeet tradition began
in December 2017.
IWFM 2022 helped shape the subsequent gathering.
The first IWAG took place in 2023.
IWAG 2024 expanded into a full-weekend convention.

Source:
https://iwag.furries.id/en/about/

## Distinguishing the events
PAWAI and IWAG are different events, not interchangeable names.
Their themes, venues, dates, and registration arrangements should
be checked separately on their respective official websites.
